# Summary (Courtesy of Kit)
The party met up in front of the SIlver Scepter. Clint spotted an unusual movement of guards towards the docks and when he asked, the guard explained that they were moving towards a fleet of nonhostile unknown ships that had showed up. The party decided to not split up and go meet the mysterious person who had called them here instead. Inside the resturant, that person turned out to be Ingar, one of the goliath brothers from the village. He explains that the villagers were seized and the boat they arrived on has been guarded by the royal guard ever since. During this time, knights of Timeor (wearing blue armor with an hourglass insignia) showed up at the resturant, searching for him. Damseol and Woudly pretended to be a couple to distract the knight while Vryn, Clint, Hayes led Ingar to the bathrooms and helped him escape through a window. He said he'd escape to the green quarter and would wait there as long as he can. He originally wanted to meet them at dusk but Hayes dgaf about this dude anymore. We finished our very expensive meal and also had to pay for Ingar's portion and tipped the waiter for being such a g. The knights of Timeor had told Damseol about a rally that Oreus and the duke would attend. The party decided to head there, but on the way stopped in front of the guarded ship and at the adventurers' guild. Upon arriving at the ship, we assessed that there was a gap small enough for Wouldy to jump onto or for Damseol to send her familiar in, but that it was too risky to try anything in broad daylight and Damseol did not have the time to summon her familiar. At the adventurer's guild we discovered we could find a missing child (10 gold), clear out a bandit gang (8 gold) or hunt creatures in the sewers (2 gold + silver per creature head we can bring back)

When we arrived at the rally, we found it pretty heavily guarded, about 20% guards that was further evenly split between the knights of Timeor and the royal guard with the rest of the populace being heavily business owners. We also noticed a portion of the crowd were the original people of Thult, before Essia was sold to Wartontia. Oreus gave a propaganda-filled speech to the people in which he pretended William was still alive and everyone needed to rally against him. Hayes got the bright idea to test Oreus's protection and stuck his tail under a random man's foot. When the man stepped on him, Oreus seemed to wince and paused in his speech. He then planted a dagger on the dude before running him through the chest and making a point that "we" had enemies hidden everywhere. Vrynn was able to deduce that this was probably meant to be equally a show to Hayes, that Oreus doesn't care about killing people but also a show of power to the crowd. We also noticed that during the entire event, the elven Duke did not speak and the Earl was nowhere to be seen. After the event, the party split up, with Vrynn, Wouldy, and Clint heading to the blacksmith. Clint negotiated with a blacksmith to get his armor reinforced with the pieces from Merric's armor, trading in his crossbow and getting lent 2 gold by Wouldy. Damseol was originally going to head back to the hotel by Hayes pulled her aside to speak. The two of them discussed what to do with Clint's pact and Damseol appologized for snapping at him when they fought Merric. After making Damseol pinky promise she'd work with him to try and oppose Oreus while keeping information from Clint so he wouldn't face backlash from his pact, Hayes explained that because of Zena's condition, he has to try and figure out how to oppose Oreus and save her before she dies. As they're talking, Damseol notices the road go from brick to cobblestone as they shift back in time.

The two of them notice they're in past Thult which has been turned into a battlefield. A way down the road, they notice a tall woman with long white hair, wearing a mask and half plate and wielding a huge scimitar standing over another figure. She's surrounded by an orange glow and seems able to sense their fear, charging towards them with her sword drawn. As Hayes steps between her and Damseol, he notices the resemblence between her and Clint and asks if she's Clint's mother, the Taras. Though she doesn't recognize Clint's name, she seems surprised that they know what a Taras is and accepts that they are time travelers when she connects it to Fel's plan. She leads them to a clearing filled with bodies surrounding a severely injured tiefling named Cedric. Damseol recognizes the tiefling as her father and tearfully approaches him, however, he doesn't recognize her or know that he has any children. He does however recognize Hayes and tells him he's seen him before and that Hayes has met him, Felaern, and an orcish lady named Ayana before. Damseol recognizes Ayana, but Cedric does not tell her how he knows her. Taras tells Hayes to carry Cedric to a safe place and fetch supplies so they can sew him back up. While Hayes searches for supplies, Damseol has a heart to heart with her father. He tells her their last name in exchange for Damseol trading the necklace that he technically gave her before for a magic pipe Timeor gave him, that bestows changing magical boons on the wearer, like the ability to see magic items. Damseol is secretly dissapointed as she wanted to keep the necklace, but doesn't express this to Cedric. Damseol tries to ask Cedric about her mom, but he doesn't have any idea why he would have a child or with who, as the baby gambit hasn't been planned yet. He also isn't quite sure what's happening, just that he and Taras (which means time protector) are buying time while Timeor and Felaern make something (likely the artifact we're searching for)

When Hayes returns with medical supplies and two bottles of top shelf liqour bc he's having a gay crisis pt2 courtesy of Cedric, Damseol is able to stitch Cedric up and the four of them made their way towards the sewers. Hayes started interogating Cedric, trying to get as much information as possible while he could. He was able to learn that there used to be four continents in total, one huge one in the northwest and one that was series of islands in the southwest that Gai-ana sunk in open opposition of the other gods. He also mentioned that Timeor's involvement was also to be kept a secret. He didn't know why the gods were doing this, only that it started 40 or 50 years ago in the north and it has to do with an old wound because they went where they shouldn't have gone. This matches what Hayes was taught growing up, that the gods opened an old wound and were corrupted as a result. He also mentioned that the artifact we were looking for is probably location in an old cave/mine system that people don't usually go to and marked it on Hayes's map. When Hayes asked Cedric about time travel sickness, he said that people should get better in a week and that otherwise, there's no cure or treatment. It shouldn't be possible for Oreus to treat her and if he's rewinding time on her, he's actually preventing her from healing. It's likely she knows something and Oreus is trying to keep her in stasis. After this they made their way into the sewer and as Damseol and Hayes entered, they shifted back to present day.
