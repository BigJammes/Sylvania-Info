# Summary (Courtesy of Kit)
After regrouping at the hotel to discuss what they will do in the coming days, the party is met with a search party sent on behalf of the Earl and Oreus. After some talk, the party has their magic items and weapons confiscated until they can provide adventurers guild registration, or leave the city (even though it seems nobody can leave the city). Woody is able to make his escape with some of the items, including the hourglass to weaken Oreus by jumping out of a window. Hayes and Damseol then decide to contact Oreus to tell him to let the party keep their items, to which he refuses unless they provide him with the items that Woody ran off with. The party, after lengthy discussion, ultimately decides against this and make plans to escape the city through the sewers. We begin the next session about to enact this plan.
