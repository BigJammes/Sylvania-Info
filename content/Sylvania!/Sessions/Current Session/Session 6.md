# Summary (Courtesy of James)
Our party has now gotten comfortable at the Gilded Amphora, but that wouldn't last for long. The morning of the 14th of Kythorn, a man named Oreus knocked on the party's hotel room door. He expressed his want for the party to stop their investigation into the whereabouts of the artifact on Essia. The party decided to continue their search anyways, driven by their want to get the reward from the Kingdom of Wartontia. Before anything else could happen though, a note was slipped under the room's door, the note had the name of a popular restaurant, and two numbers (2 and 5). The group determined that the second number was a time to meet them, and the third might be a booth or seat number.
After this encounter the party decided to eat breakfast then leave the hotel, splitting into three groups. Clint and Hayes traveled to the nearby Venseil temple, Woody and Vrynn went to see if they could sell some of their ancient gold coins, and Damseol went to buy spell components. Damseol ended up stealing the components while the owner of the shop was seemingly out for lunch. Woody and Vrynn meanwhile failed to sell the coins for a price they liked, and decided they would search around town. Hayes and Clint had a pretty interesting encounter at the Temple, which had seemingly been repurposed as a triage center for injured refugees. Hayes reunited with his close friend Zena, who seemed to be in a comatose state, and still quite injured. There Hayes met the cleric Theseus, who explained that he had found Zena in her injured state. Meanwhile, Clint was helping the head paladin Oridor carry in some supplies. It was at this point that Oreus appeared and entered the temple. He made his way up to the second floor, tailed by Clint who was asking him questions about his motives. On the second floor, Oreus began treating Zena while Hayes and Clint waited outside of the room. Afterwards, Clint and Hayes spoke with Oreus who talked Clint into making a pact with him. The pact was quite vague, but the basics of it were that if anybody in Clint's group were to be injured inside of Thult, then Oreus would come to protect them. However, Clint had to inform Oreus whenever *any* member of his group left Thult. After this the party regrouped and Clint informed everyone of the pact, and Damseol was quite upset.
