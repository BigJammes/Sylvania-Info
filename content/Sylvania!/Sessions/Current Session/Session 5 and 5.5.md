# Summary (Courtesy of Kira)
The group reconvened after victoriously fighting off Esther with the Iron Corsairs. Hayes took charge in debriefing Merrick all that we had been through thus far, while Wouldy and Damseol inquired about the “secret stash” that they had found that was mentioned in Oscar’s note to Olvar. Merrick confirmed the existence of said stash and gave them a mysterious box containing a key that belonged to a private library in Thult and a very strange coin that had etchings on it that were unfamiliar. While opening the box mid conversation with Wouldy and Merrick, Damseol experienced a vision of two men, an elf and a halfing in front of some sort of vault that was etched in ancient runes. They briefly spoke of enacting a plan soon and were talking in an old form of sylvan. The last thing Damseol saw before she was brought back was the skirt of a very well made dress belonging to an unknown person. The group finishes up with getting themselves and the villagers in order, Merrick jumps to action and out of nowhere goes to kill Esther. Unfortunately, the group was not fast enough to thwart his sudden attack and he ultimately kills her, saying that she was calling for back up and they needed to move out. Now needing to move with a sense of urgency, the group decided to have the villagers and select members of the Iron Corsairs accompany them and Aurelius while the party, Merrick and his monk would all go to a nearby temple of Timior that was being used as a hideout for the pirates. (edited)
After much exploring, the party was able to retrieve some important items from the shrine and Clint fell upon another “vision” of the same elven man and now another Tiefling man. They seemed to interact with Clint, asking about what happened in the future with Clint and being shocked at the Dragons being subjugated, going as far as questioning if they failed at what they were attempting to do. After a couple more words, they left Clint with another coin and a journal, then Clint was brought back with more questions being brought up now than being answered— especially about their relation with his mother of all people. Continuing their search in the temple, the party finally came across Oscar and a multitude of his lackeys. There was a struggle, but inevitably the party was able to shift the tides in their favor, that is until Oscar channeled arcane energy within himself to call William Whitehair himself to their very location. They were given a very cryptic warning of “stay out of sight”, and with that everybody scrambled to get onto the ship in one piece. Williams caught up to everybody and immediately set his sights on Aurelius, who he needed alive. Assuming the worst, Aurelius prepared to leave his belongings with the group, however the fight still continued anyways with eventually, by the luck of the gods it would seem, Williams perishing in the fray. The group set sail for Thult immediately after, now celebrating their victory and the mark of the end of the reign of terror Williams had placed on the local lands.
