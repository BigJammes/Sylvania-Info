**Type:** Casual Tavern-Style Eatery  
**Description:** Favored by sailors, traders, and locals alike, The Salty Siren offers hearty, rustic fare in a warm, bustling atmosphere. Its straightforward, flavor-forward dishes evoke the spirit of ancient coastal taverns.

**Menu:**

- **Appetizer – Siren’s Seafood Stew:**  
    A robust broth brimming with mussels, clams, shrimp, ripe tomatoes, garlic, and local herbs, served with freshly baked crusty bread.
- **Main Course – Tide-Tossed Grilled Fish Wrap:**  
    Lightly charred fillets wrapped in flatbread with a tangy fennel-tomato salsa.
- **Side – Garden Medley Skewer:**  
    Skewers of seasonal vegetables—zucchini, bell peppers, and red onions—grilled and drizzled with extra virgin olive oil.
- **Dessert – Lemon Olive Oil Cake:**  
    A moist, zesty cake finished with a light honey glaze.
- **Beverage – Seabreeze Ale:**  
    A refreshing local brew with a hint of citrus to complement the seafood fare.
