**Type:** Upscale Waterfront Dining  
**Description:** Perched along Thult’s bustling harbor, The Golden Trident is renowned for its refined presentation of fresh seafood and produce. Elegant yet approachable, it attracts nobles and visiting dignitaries seeking a taste of Essia’s finest flavors.

**Menu:**

- **Starter – Aegean Aphrodisiac Salad:**  
    Mixed baby greens, halved cherry tomatoes, Kalamata olives, and lightly marinated shrimp, all tossed in a citrus-olive oil dressing.
- **Main Course – Trident’s Catch:**  
    Oven-roasted sea bass infused with thyme and lemon, served atop a bed of herb-polenta and accompanied by charred asparagus.
- **Vegetarian Option – Poseidon’s Garden Medley:**  
    A colorful array of grilled zucchini, eggplant, and bell peppers finished with a drizzle of basil pesto and a sprinkle of crumbled feta.
- **Dessert – Honeyed Fig Tart:**  
    A delicate pastry filled with local figs and wildflower honey, served with a dollop of basil-infused cream.
- **Beverage – Elysian White Wine:**  
    A crisp, citrus-kissed wine sourced from Essia’s own vineyards.
