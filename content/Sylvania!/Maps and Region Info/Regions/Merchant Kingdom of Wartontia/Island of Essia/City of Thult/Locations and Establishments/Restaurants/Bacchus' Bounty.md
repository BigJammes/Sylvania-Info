**Type:** Mid-Range
**Description:** Celebrating both the harvest and the ocean’s gifts, Bacchus’ Bounty blends inspired elegance with vibrant, communal flavors. Ideal for gatherings and feasts, it boasts hearty dishes that are as satisfying as they are artfully presented.

**Menu:**

- **Starter – Roman Sea Salad:**  
    Crisp mixed greens topped with tender, marinated octopus, sliced red onions, olives, and a zesty lemon vinaigrette.
- **Main Course – Spiced Sea Bass à la Bacchus:**  
    Pan-seared sea bass fillet seasoned with rosemary and garlic, served alongside roasted root vegetables and a drizzle of olive oil.
- **Vegetarian Option – Herbaceous Vegetable Ragout:**  
    A slow-cooked stew of tomatoes, eggplant, zucchini, and bell peppers, richly flavored with local herbs.
- **Dessert – Grapes & Honey Panna Cotta:**  
    A smooth, creamy panna cotta accented with a reduction of local grapes and a generous drizzle of honey.
- **Beverage – Bacchus’ Mulled Wine:**  
    A warmly spiced red wine infused with cinnamon and citrus—a perfect complement to a cool Essian evening.
