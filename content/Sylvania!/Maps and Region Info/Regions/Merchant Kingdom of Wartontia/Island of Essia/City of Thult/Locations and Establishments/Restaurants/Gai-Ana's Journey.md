**Type:** Fusion Dining (Elegant & Rustic)  
**Description:** Honoring both the bounties of the earth and the sea, Gai-Ana's Journey artfully combines robust, earthy flavors with delicate maritime influences. The décor is a harmonious blend of terracotta and sea-blue accents, evoking ancient temples and coastal groves.

**Menu:**

- **Appetizer – Gai-Ana's Bounty:**  
    A curated selection of grilled calamari, shrimp, and mussels paired with a crisp fennel and orange salad.
- **Main Course – Bountiful Harvest Bowl:**  
    A nourishing bowl featuring mixed greens, roasted bell peppers, artichoke hearts, and tender chunks of grilled fish, all drizzled with an olive tapenade.
- **Side – Fennel & Citrus Salad:**  
    Thinly sliced fennel with juicy orange segments and pomegranate seeds, lightly tossed in an herbed dressing.
- **Dessert – Baked Apple with Honey-Almond Crumble:**  
    Warm baked apple slices topped with a crunchy almond crumble and served with a side of basil-infused whipped cream.
- **Beverage – Gai-Ana’s Sparkling Nectar:**  
    A refreshingly effervescent drink blending local citrus juices and mineral water.
