**Type:** Lively Outdoor Eatery 
**Description:** For adventurers and locals on the go, Trios' Treats is a vibrant street-side eatery that captures the essence of Essia’s seaside flavors. Its fast, flavorful dishes are perfect for a quick bite during a day of exploration.

**Menu:**

- **Snack – Sea Breeze Skewers:**  
    Bite-sized shrimp and seasonal vegetable skewers, grilled to perfection and served with a zesty garlic-herb dipping sauce.
- **Main Course – Rustic Sea Soup:**  
    A light yet flavorful broth filled with clams, tender fish chunks, and a medley of garden-fresh vegetables.
- **Side – Tide-Tossed Salad:**  
    A refreshing mix of chopped greens, diced citrus segments, and capers, tossed in a bright lemon dressing.
- **Dessert – Fresh Fruit Medley:**  
    An assortment of seasonal fruits drizzled with honey and sprinkled with toasted seeds.
- **Beverage – Seaside Lemonade:**  
    A tangy, invigorating lemonade with a hint of fresh mint.
