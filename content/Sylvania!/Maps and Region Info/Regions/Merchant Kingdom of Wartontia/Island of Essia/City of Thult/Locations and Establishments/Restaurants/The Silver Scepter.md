### **Appetizers**

**Crowned Calamari**  
Lightly seared calamari rings drizzled with a citrus-mint aioli, garnished with edible silver leaf and micro-herbs.

### **Main Courses**

**Scepter’s Symphony**  
An artful ensemble of grilled scallops, tender calamari, and succulent shrimp atop saffron-infused risotto, finished with a delicate drizzle of citrus reduction and a garnish of fresh basil.

### **Desserts**

**Celestial Honey Panna Cotta**  
A velvety panna cotta gently set with wildflower honey, served with a warm compote of locally gathered berries and a sprig of fresh mint.

### **Beverages**

**The Silver Nectar**  
A rare vintage white wine with subtle hints of citrus and mineral notes, carefully selected to complement the ocean’s bounty.
